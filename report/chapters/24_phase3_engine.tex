\section{Phase 3 --- The dependency-free C++ engine}
\label{ch:phase3}

\textbf{Reproduce it:} \code{cmake -S src -B build \&\& cmake --build build -j2}, then
\code{./build/statarbsim --version}, \code{--test}, \code{--demo}, and
\code{./build/microbench}.\\
\textbf{Math used:} Sections~\ref{sec:ols:incremental}, \ref{sec:ols:cholesky},
\ref{sec:pca:jacobi}; Chapter~\ref{ch:systems}.\\
\textbf{Artifacts:} \code{src/} (11 headers, 1{,}255 lines, plus \code{main.cpp}),
\code{test/minimal\_test.hpp} + \code{test/engine\_tests.cpp} (610 lines, 24 tests),
\code{bench/microbench.cpp}, \code{src/CMakeLists.txt}.

\subsection{Goal}

A compiler-verified, allocation-free, latency-instrumented simulation core with the
required architecture --- before any strategy result exists. Building the engine first,
with its tests, is what makes the later phases' numbers credible: the harness is fixed and
audited before it is allowed to produce results.

\subsection{The test suite: properties, not values}

\code{./build/statarbsim --test} runs \textbf{24 tests with 2{,}852 assertions}. The suite
is organised around invariants that would catch a real bug rather than around expected
numbers:

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\small
\begin{tabular}{p{0.42\linewidth}p{0.52\linewidth}}
\toprule
Test & Property asserted \\
\midrule
\code{buffer\_basic\_and\_contiguous} & SoA buffers hold what was pushed; \code{data()} pointers are contiguous; capacity is respected \\
\code{no\_allocation\_in\_hot\_push\_path} & a global \code{operator new} counter stays at \textbf{0} across 1{,}000 windowed updates+solves (Section~\ref{sec:sys:alloc}) \\
\code{spd\_solve} & Cholesky reconstructs $LL^{\top}=A$ and solves $A\beta=b$ to $10^{-12}$ \\
\code{ridge\_inverts\_singular} & a rank-deficient $X^{\top}X$ becomes solvable with $\lambda>0$ --- the numerical claim of Section~\ref{sec:ridge} \\
\code{symmetric\_eig} & Jacobi gives $V^{\top}V=I$ and $AV=V\Lambda$ with $\lambda$ sorted descending \\
\code{rolling\_regression\_matches\_bruteforce} & incremental $\hat\beta$ equals a from-scratch refit to $10^{-8}$ at every window position (Section~\ref{sec:sys:accuracy}) \\
\code{rolling\_eviction\_matches\_recomputed\_sliding} & the evicted row is the right row: accumulator equals a recomputed sliding window over the whole series \\
\code{window\_not\_yet\_full} & before $W$ bars the estimator reports ``not ready'' rather than fitting on a short window --- the edge case that silently produces wild betas at the start of a backtest \\
\code{signal\_bands}, \code{signal\_half\_life\_cap} & entry/exit hysteresis behaves as \eqref{eq:saa:policy}; the holding cap binds \\
\code{book\_exposures} & gross/net exposure arithmetic on a signed weight vector \\
\code{latency\_percentiles}, \code{engine\_runs\_and\_measures\_latency} & the histogram's quantiles are monotone in $q$ and lie inside the configured range; a full engine run produces non-zero sample counts per stage \\
\code{backtest\_one\_bar\_delay\_no\_lookahead} & \textbf{the lookahead test}: a single manufactured mispricing never produces an active position on the signal day itself \\
\code{backtest\_no\_trade\_when\_too\_short} & no trade is possible before the window is warm \\
\code{backtest\_costs\_never\_improve\_net} & net P\&L $\le$ gross P\&L for every $c\ge0$, bar by bar --- a sign-error detector for the cost model \\
\code{inverse\_vol\_weights}, \code{risk\_gross\_net\_limits} & weights sum to 1 and order inversely to $\sigma$; limits verification catches a violation and passes a conforming book \\
\code{selector\_ols\_recovers\_beta} & on synthetic data with a known $\beta$, OLS recovers it \\
\code{selector\_ridge\_tends\_to\_ols} & $\hat\beta_{\text{ridge}}\to\hat\beta_{\text{OLS}}$ as $\lambda\to0$ (the analytic limit of \eqref{eq:ridge:bias}) \\
\code{selector\_lasso\_sparsifies} & coefficients become exactly $0$ as $\lambda$ grows --- the KKT claim of \eqref{eq:lasso_kkt}, tested numerically \\
\code{selector\_en\_between\_ridge\_and\_lasso} & elastic-net solutions interpolate between the two penalties \\
\code{selector\_pcr\_all\_components\_equals\_ols} & $\mathrm{PCR}(k=n)\equiv\mathrm{OLS}$ exactly (the identity of Section~\ref{sec:pcr}) \\
\code{selector\_pcr\_reduces\_with\_fewer\_components} & truncating components changes the fit in the predicted direction \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The design principle: each test asserts a \emph{mathematical identity} from Part~I or a
\emph{safety property} of the simulation. Identities survive refactoring; hardcoded
expected values do not. And a test that asserts a limit ($\lambda\to0$, $k=n$, $c\ge0$)
catches a whole class of bugs that a point-value test would miss.

\subsection{Measured output of this phase}

The latency demo (\code{--demo}, 30{,}000 synthetic bars) and the microbenchmarks were
measured here for the first time and are re-measured on every report build. Current live
numbers (Table~\ref{tab:engine_latency}, Table~\ref{tab:engine_bench}):
regression update p50 $\approx600$\,ns, signal generation $\approx21$\,ns, order
placement $\approx21$\,ns; \code{vector} $1.08$--$1.33\times$ \code{deque}; SoA
$1.03$--$1.51\times$ AoS (ranges across builds; the live values are in
\code{results/tables/engine\_bench.csv}).
The honest interpretation of the two multipliers --- they are modest because both
comparisons fit in cache at these sizes --- is in Sections~\ref{sec:sys:soa}
and~\ref{sec:sys:vector}; the phase-level point is that the measurement exists, is
repeatable, and is not inflated.

\subsection{Honest reading}

The engine is small (1{,}255 lines of headers) and deliberately so: it implements exactly
the primitives the strategy needs, nothing else. Three limitations are worth stating
rather than hiding.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{The Kalman filter is not in C++.} It lives in Python
    (\code{scripts/model/kalman.py}). Per-bar cost is $\lesssim250$ flops, so the port
    would change no conclusion; it is deferred, and the deferral is documented in
    Phase~\ref{ch:phase8} rather than left implicit.
  \item \textbf{The C++ estimators are cross-checked but not the source of the reported
    numbers.} Phases~\ref{ch:phase5}--\ref{ch:phase9} report the sklearn/Python walk-forward
    results; the C++ \code{basket\_selector} implements the same five estimators and is
    unit-tested against the same identities, and the Phase~\ref{ch:phase4} backtest is run
    by both paths with agreement to three decimals on net Sharpe. Two implementations of
    the same experiment is a strength; claiming the C++ path produced the report's tables
    would be false.
  \item \textbf{The latency histogram's resolution} ($\times1.158$ per bucket) makes
    p50/p95/p99 collapse to one value when the distribution is tight
    (Section~\ref{sec:sys:measurement}). Reported as measured, with the reason.
\end{enumerate}

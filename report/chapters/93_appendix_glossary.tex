\subsection{Glossary of terms}

\begin{description}
  \item[Adjusted close] A price series adjusted for splits and dividends, so that its
    returns are total returns. Used for all return series in this project.
  \item[Bonferroni correction] The most conservative multiplicity adjustment: compare each
    $p$-value to $\alpha/M$, where $M$ is the number of hypotheses tested.
  \item[Breadth] The number of independent opportunities a strategy exploits. In
    Grinold's law, $IR=IC\sqrt{\text{breadth}}$.
  \item[Cointegration] A long-run equilibrium relation between non-stationary series whose
    linear combination is stationary. Distinct from correlation, which is a statement about
    co-movement of differences.
  \item[Conditional value at risk (CVaR)] The expected loss given that the loss exceeds
    \code{VaR}. Coherent, unlike \code{VaR}.
  \item[Cost model] The function mapping a trade to its cash cost: commission, spread,
    impact and (here) an optional slippage multiplier.
  \item[Data snooping] The bias introduced by selecting the best of many strategies after
    seeing the data. Quantified in this project by the \code{M=44} audit.
  \item[Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR)] The probability that a strategy's Sharpe ratio exceeds
    the benchmark implied by the number of trials, after correcting for skewness and
    kurtosis.
  \item[Diebold--Mariano (DM) test] A test of equal predictive accuracy between two
    forecasts, using a HAC variance of the loss differential.
  \item[Drawdown] The decline from a running peak of an equity curve. The maximum drawdown
    is the largest such decline in the sample.
  \item[Engle--Granger procedure] Estimate the cointegrating vector by OLS, then test the
    residual for a unit root; rejection implies cointegration.
  \item[Filter / deadband] A no-trade band inside which signals are ignored, used to
    suppress trading on noise.
  \item[Flop-per-byte ratio ($\eta$)] Arithmetic intensity: floating-point operations per
    byte moved between memory and cache. Determines whether a kernel is compute- or
    memory-bound.
  \item[GARCH] A model of time-varying volatility; mentioned in Sec.~\ref{sec:ts:heterosk} as
    not implemented here.
  \item[Gross / net] Before / after transaction costs. A strategy quoted only gross has
    not been tested against the thing that usually kills it.
  \item[HAC (Newey--West)] Heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation consistent covariance
    estimation. Inflates standard errors when residuals are serially correlated.
  \item[Half-life] The time for a mean-reverting process to close half the gap to its
    mean: $\tau_{1/2}=\log 2/\theta$.
  \item[Hedged portfolio] A book with the market factor neutralised (here, beta $\approx0$
    by construction and by the \code{portfolio\_summary} audit).
  \item[In-sample / out-of-sample (IS / OOS)] Estimated on the training window / evaluated
    on the held-out window. All headline numbers here are OOS.
  \item[Johansen test] A maximum-likelihood test of the cointegration rank of a vector
    system; gives the number of independent cointegrating relations.
  \item[Kalman filter] A recursive minimum-variance estimator of a latent state from noisy
    observations. Used here for a time-varying hedge.
  \item[KPSS test] Tests the null of stationarity; complements the ADF test, whose null is
    a unit root. Agreement of both is required for a confident verdict.
  \item[Look-ahead bias] Using information at time $t$ that was not available until after
    $t$. Eliminated here by the strict \code{train/test} split and walk-forward estimation.
  \item[Maximum holding period] A hard stop that closes a position after $h_{\max}$ bars
    regardless of signal, bounding tail exposure.
  \item[Newey--West bandwidth] The number of lags $L$ included in the HAC estimator;
    $L\to\infty$ recovers OLS variance, $L=0$ recovers White's estimator.
  \item[Non-stationarity] A property of a series whose mean, variance or autocovariance
    depends on time. Financial prices are typically non-stationary; their returns are not.
  \item[Ornstein--Uhlenbeck (OU) process] The continuous-time mean-reverting Gaussian
    process; the theoretical model of a cointegrated spread.
  \item[Participation ratio (PR)] $\left(\sum\lambda_k\right)^2/\sum\lambda_k^2$: the
    effective number of equally-weighted independent directions in a covariance matrix.
  \item[Plateau] The region of the parameter surface where performance is insensitive to
    the parameter; the opposite of a sharp optimum, which is a sign of overfitting.
  \item[Power] The probability that a test rejects a false null. Low power means an
    inability to detect an effect, not evidence of its absence.
  \item[Reality Check / SPA] Bootstrap tests of the null that the best of $M$ strategies
    has no predictive superiority over a benchmark. Both fail to reject here.
  \item[RESET test] Ramsey's regression specification error test: augment the model with
    powers of the fitted values and test their joint significance.
  \item[Sharpe ratio] Mean excess return divided by its standard deviation, annualised by
    $\sqrt{252}$ for daily data. Its sampling variance depends on skewness and kurtosis.
  \item[Slippage multiplier] A factor applied to the per-side cost to stress-test
    execution quality; swept in \code{backtest\_summary}.
  \item[Spurious regression] A regression between two independent non-stationary series
    that appears highly significant; the canonical failure of ignoring unit roots.
  \item[Stationary bootstrap] A resampling scheme with random (geometric) block lengths
    that preserves the mean block length while producing stationary resamples.
  \item[Structure-of-arrays (SoA)] A memory layout storing each field in its own contiguous
    array; the opposite of array-of-structures (AoS).
  \item[Variance inflation factor (VIF)] $1/(1-R_j^2)$: how much the variance of a
    coefficient is inflated by collinearity with the other regressors.
  \item[Walk-forward] Estimating on a trailing window and trading the next bar, rolling
    forward in time; the operational form of no look-ahead.
\end{description}

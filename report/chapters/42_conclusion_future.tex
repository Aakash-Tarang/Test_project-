\section{Conclusion and future work}
\label{ch:conclusion}

\subsection{The answer to the question the project was built to answer}

\emph{Does a statistically defensible statistical-arbitrage edge exist in this universe,
after costs, out-of-sample validation, and correction for the breadth of the search?}

\textbf{No --- not at this frequency, this breadth, and this cost level.} The precise
statement, with the numbers that support it:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item No strategy in the audited set of $M=44$ rejects $H_0$: mean daily net P\&L
    $\le0$ at the Bonferroni-corrected level $\alpha/M=0.001136$; \textbf{zero} survive.
    The best raw one-sided HAC $p$-value in the entire set is $0.0587$, which does not
    clear even the uncorrected 5\% bar.
  \item The bootstrap data-snooping tests, which account for the cross-strategy dependence
    that Bonferroni ignores, agree: White's Reality Check $p=0.868$, Hansen's SPA
    $p=0.638$.
  \item The best observed Sharpe ($0.346$) is \emph{below} the expected maximum of 44
    noise strategies given their realized dispersion ($\mathrm{SR}_0=0.474$), the deflated
    Sharpe ratio is $0.305$ against a $>0.95$ bar, and the stationary-bootstrap 95\%
    interval for it, $[-0.058,\ +0.753]$, contains zero.
  \item No pairwise comparison of hedge estimators is statistically separable: all ten
    Diebold--Mariano $p$-values lie in $[0.22,0.96]$ and every HAC confidence interval
    spans zero.
  \item The four-strategy book is net-negative over the full sample (net Sharpe $-0.401$
    inverse-vol, $-0.463$ equal-weight), and the single-basket baseline is net $+0.050$
    against a standard error of $\approx0.245$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{What is nevertheless established}

The negative verdict applies to the \emph{magnitude of the traded Sharpe}. It does not
apply to the structure, which is established by tests that do not involve selecting a
maximum and is therefore immune to the multiplicity argument:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Adaptive estimation creates the tradeable object.} The rolling-hedge spread
    rejects a unit root decisively (ADF $p<10^{-3}$, half-life $9.4$ bars, stable across
    2010--2026 and 2022+); the static sixteen-year hedge does not ($p=0.083$, half-life
    $171$--$476$ bars). Mean reversion in this universe is a property of an
    \emph{adaptively estimated} relationship, not of a fixed long-run one. That is a real,
    non-obvious, and reproducible econometric finding.
  \item \textbf{The basket is genuinely cointegrated} (Johansen rank $r=2$; Engle--Granger
    $p=0.003$ and $0.033$ for two of three legs) and the dominant hedge weights survive
    HAC correction ($t=7.3$, $9.4$) --- while naive inference would have overstated them by
    a factor of four.
  \item \textbf{The signal has out-of-sample directional content} (IC $0.125$--$0.211$ on a
    pre-specified 2022+ test window, positive for all five linear estimators and for the
    Kalman innovation).
  \item \textbf{Cost, not signal, is the binding constraint.} $87\%$ of the gross return is
    paid away at 10\,bps one-way; break-even is $\approx11$\,bps; every improvement found
    (ridge, smooth Kalman, higher entry threshold, longer window) works by reducing
    turnover. The sensitivity surface's shape is predicted in advance by
    \eqref{eq:costdrag} and \eqref{eq:ou:profit} and confirmed by measurement.
  \item \textbf{Linear beats nonlinear, for a reason that is a property of the sample and
    not of the market.} The GBM's learning curve is still negative at $2{,}400$ of the
    $\approx2{,}881$ available training bars; RESET does not reject linearity on the main
    basket; and no nonlinear entry gate improves net Sharpe.
  \item \textbf{The engineering claims are measured and traceable.} 24 C++ tests /
    2{,}852 assertions, 35 statistical unit tests, 52/52 exit-gate checks, zero allocations
    in the hot path, incremental regression equal to brute force at $10^{-8}$, and every
    systems number captured from a live run into a CSV.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Under what conditions would the answer change?}

Three, in order of expected value (Chapter~\ref{ch:nongoals} gives the operational detail):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{More breadth, tested as a book.} $\mathrm{IR}=\mathrm{IC}\sqrt{B}$
    \eqref{eq:fundamentallaw}: the same per-basket IC of $\approx0.15$--$0.2$ across 200
    disjoint baskets implies a book Sharpe of $2$--$3$ before costs and, crucially, a
    \emph{single} hypothesis to test rather than 200. This converts an unfalsifiable
    experiment into a falsifiable one, and the engine's $\approx600$\,ns per-bar cost
    already supports the required $10^6$-backtest scan.
  \item \textbf{Higher frequency.} $T^\ast=(z/\mathrm{SR}_d)^2$ is measured in bars; moving
    from daily to hourly multiplies the annual sample by $\approx7$ and shortens half-lives
    by one to two orders of magnitude, which is the same effect as breadth but on the time
    axis.
  \item \textbf{Lower cost per unit of turnover.} Break-even is $\approx11$\,bps one-way at
    the current turnover. Halving the effective cost --- by trading less often, by crossing
    spreads rather than paying them, or by netting within the book --- roughly doubles the
    net Sharpe of every variant measured here, without any new signal.
\end{enumerate}
What would \emph{not} change the answer: more hyper-parameter search, more baskets searched
post hoc, a more flexible model on the same features, or reporting gross rather than net
numbers. All four increase $M$ faster than they increase power.

\subsection{What this project is, stated plainly}

It is not a strategy and it is not a claim of alpha. It is a complete, reproducible,
adversarially audited implementation of the basket statistical-arbitrage idea --- data
pipeline with a ten-check quality gate, a unit-tested statistical library, a
dependency-free C++ simulation engine with property-based correctness tests, five hedge
estimators and an adaptive state-space estimator compared through one identical causal
cost-aware harness, the full diagnostic battery on the residuals actually traded, a
four-strategy book with enforced and verified risk limits, a nonlinear extension with an
honest negative verdict, a 44-hypothesis data-snooping audit with bootstrap corrections and
a power analysis, and a book-length report generated from the results by code.

Its empirical conclusion is negative and its methodological conclusion is positive: the
edge's \emph{structure} reproduces, its \emph{magnitude} does not survive honest
accounting, and the reason is quantitative and known --- not enough independent bets, at too
high a cost per bet, in a sample whose own standard error is five times the effect being
measured. A negative result delivered this way is worth more than a positive one delivered
without the audit, because it tells you exactly what to change: breadth, frequency, or
cost. Everything in this repository is built to make that next experiment cheap to run.
